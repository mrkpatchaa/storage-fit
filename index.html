    How to read it

    • Front is the default view, followed by the draggable 3D view.
    • Top view is useful for checking exact drawer-floor placement.
    • Use Edit layout to drag or nudge boxes in Top view. Arrow keys move by one snap step; Shift + Arrow moves by five.
    • Exact position lets you type X from the inside left edge, Y from the inside front edge, and Z from the floor.
    • Quick align snaps the selected organizer to an edge or center while still respecting collisions and stack support.
    • Undo / Redo tracks up to 60 successful edits. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl/Cmd+Y redoes.
    • Replace organizer swaps a selected placement while keeping its purpose label and position when possible.
    • Use Move to floor / Stack on support to change elevation without rebuilding the layout.
    • Mirror L↔R or F↔B flips the whole proposal and only keeps it if the mirrored result is still physically valid.
    • Give individual organizers a purpose such as Socks or Cables.
    • Striped red shapes are blocked zones and are never used by the optimizer.
    • Stacked items show their elevation in Top view; Front, Side and 3D use their real vertical position.
